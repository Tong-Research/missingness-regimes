# R1 — re-root the hierarchy at the imputed model — pre-registered 2026-09-02 13:45, BEFORE any run

## The diagnosis this tests

The method loses to mean imputation because partitioning the data by pattern costs rows: each
per-pattern logistic regression is fitted on $|\mathcal{S}(e)| \ge 30$ rows against $|e|$
coefficients (median support-to-dimension ratio $5.5$ on Colorectal, $19$–$35$ elsewhere), while
mean imputation fits one model on all $40{,}000$ rows. Ancestor shrinkage regularises those fits
but cannot supply rows. And the $\kappa \to \infty$ limit does not rescue it, because the
hierarchy's root is its *smallest* pattern — few variables, many rows — not the imputed model on
all variables. **The hierarchy is rooted at the wrong node.**

## The change

Add a virtual root $e_0 = \{1, \dots, d\}$ whose parameters $\hat\theta_{e_0}$ are the
coefficients of a logistic regression fitted on **all** training rows with column-mean imputation,
expressed in the hierarchy's own scaling so that shrinking toward it mixes commensurable units.
Every real pattern's ancestor chain now terminates at $e_0$. Nothing else changes: Eq. shrink,
Eq. lambda, `cover`, `combine`, the support weighting and the fittable threshold are as published.

Two consequences by construction: at $\kappa \to \infty$ every pattern collapses to $e_0$ and the
method **is** mean imputation + LR; and $e_0$ applies to every test row, so the no-match fallback
to the marginal is never taken.

Frozen modules untouched — this lives in `src/hgmiss/ideas/rooted.py` and reuses `shrink`,
`cover`, `combine` and `immediate_ancestors` by import.

## Arms, all sharing $(\text{seed}, \text{fold})$

| arm | what |
|---|---|
| `mean_impute` | the frozen baseline (`mean_impute_lr`) |
| `ours_asis` | the frozen estimator at its pre-specified $\kappa$ |
| `rooted_kinf` | re-rooted, $\kappa = 10^{9}$ — must equal `mean_impute` |
| `rooted_cv` | re-rooted, $\kappa$ chosen by inner CV on the training fold over the published grid $\cup \{10^{9}\}$ |

## Predictions

1. **`rooted_kinf` equals `mean_impute` to within solver tolerance** — paired median $0$, every
   cell within $10^{-4}$. This is the instrument check: if it fails, the root is not the imputed
   model and nothing else here is interpretable.
2. **`rooted_cv` is never below `mean_impute` by more than CV-selection noise** ($\ge -0.001$
   paired median) on either dev cohort. This is the "at least similar" guarantee, and it is
   arithmetic given prediction 1.
3. **`rooted_cv` beats `ours_asis`** on both dev cohorts, clearing the $0.002$ floor — because
   `ours_asis` is bounded above by a worse root.
4. **`rooted_cv` does NOT beat `mean_impute` by the floor on Lung.** The three nulls say the
   pattern carries little beyond the values.
5. **Colorectal is the one place it might**: low support, $17.9\%$ of records below the fittable
   threshold. Direction predicted positive; magnitude not predicted; the floor is the bar.
6. **Inner CV selects $\kappa = 10^{9}$ (the pure-imputation corner) on Lung** in the majority of
   folds, and an interior $\kappa$ on Colorectal in the majority — the same asymmetry as H1.

## Withdrawal conditions

- **(a) Prediction 1 fails.** Stop; the root is wrong; report nothing.
- **(b) `rooted_cv` is below `mean_impute` by $> 0.002$ paired on either cohort.** Then inner
  CV is failing to find a corner it can reach, and the CV — not the method — must be debugged
  before any number is reported.
- **(c) `rooted_cv` beats `mean_impute` by $\ge 0.002$ on BOTH dev cohorts and beats its permuted
  null by the same margin.** Then the pattern structure carries recoverable signal that the
  published root was hiding — which contradicts the nulls and must be replicated on the holdout
  (with `--unblind`, recorded) before Paper 1's discussion is touched.

## Null

Permute the mask rows within the training fold (the oracle ceiling's null: pattern set,
supports and feature distribution preserved; pattern–outcome association destroyed), rebuild the
hierarchy on the permuted patterns, re-root, and run `rooted_cv`. Real minus null is what is
reported alongside prediction 5.

## Scope

Dev cohorts Colorectal and Lung only; Ovarian and Prostate refused in code. 5 seeds × 5 folds,
$40{,}000$-record subsample, baseline features, `min_support = 30`. Levels are not comparable to
the main table and are not reported as such.
